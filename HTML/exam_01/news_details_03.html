Schools Launch Tree Planting Campaign

Several schools have launched a tree planting campaign to promote environmental protection and sustainability. Students and teachers are participating by planting saplings in school grounds and nearby public spaces with permission. The campaign also includes awareness sessions about climate change, biodiversity, and the importance of protecting forests. Organisers have encouraged students to care for the saplings regularly so that the initiative produces long-term benefits for the environment.
