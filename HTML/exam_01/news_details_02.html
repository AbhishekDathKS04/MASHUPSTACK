New AI Tool Helps Students Learn Faster

A new artificial intelligence learning tool has been introduced to make education more interactive and accessible. The platform allows students to ask questions, practise exercises, and receive explanations based on their learning needs. It can help learners revise lessons and identify areas where they need improvement. Educators believe that such tools can support classroom teaching when used responsibly. However, students are encouraged to verify information and use AI as a learning assistant rather than a replacement for independent thinking.
