Government Announces New Scholarships for Students

The government has introduced a new scholarship programme aimed at helping students from economically weaker families continue their education. The scheme will provide financial assistance to eligible students to cover educational expenses, including tuition fees, books, and other learning materials. Students can apply through the official scholarship portal by submitting the required documents. Educational institutions have been encouraged to inform eligible students about the programme and help them complete the application process.
